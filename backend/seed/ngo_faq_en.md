# Amal Foundation — Frequently Asked Questions (English)

Amal Foundation (مؤسسة أمل) is a community relief and education non-profit. This
document is the official knowledge base used by our support assistant.

## About us

**What is Amal Foundation?**
Amal Foundation is a registered non-profit organization founded in 2014. We provide
emergency relief, education support, and vocational training to underserved families
in our community.

**What is your mission?**
Our mission is to give every family access to food security, quality education, and a
path to self-reliance, regardless of background or income.

**Is Amal Foundation a registered charity?**
Yes. Amal Foundation is a registered non-profit (registration no. NP-2014-0317) and
publishes an audited annual report every year.

## Programs and services

**What programs do you run?**
We run four core programs: (1) the Food Relief program providing monthly food baskets,
(2) the Scholarship program covering school and university fees, (3) the Skills Academy
offering free vocational courses, and (4) the Family Support program offering counseling
and emergency cash assistance.

**Do you provide food assistance?**
Yes. Our Food Relief program distributes monthly food baskets to registered families.
Each basket feeds a family of five for about one month.

**Do you offer scholarships?**
Yes. The Scholarship program covers tuition, books, and transport for students from
low-income families, from primary school through university.

**What courses does the Skills Academy offer?**
The Skills Academy offers free courses in computer skills, English language, sewing and
tailoring, and small-business management. Courses run in 8-week cycles.

## Getting help

**Who is eligible for aid?**
Families living below the local poverty line, registered refugees, people with
disabilities, and households that have lost their primary income are eligible to apply
for relief and family support.

**How do I apply for assistance?**
Visit any of our offices during opening hours with an ID and proof of residence, or
submit a request through this assistant. A case worker will contact you within three
working days to complete the assessment.

**How long does an aid application take?**
Initial review takes up to three working days. If your case is approved, food relief
begins the following distribution cycle and cash assistance is issued within one week.

## Supporting us

**How can I donate?**
You can donate online at amalfoundation.org/donate, by bank transfer to our account
(IBAN published on the donate page), or in person at any office. Donations of any size
are welcome and a receipt is issued for every gift.

**How can I volunteer?**
We welcome volunteers for food distribution, tutoring, and event support. Sign up at
amalfoundation.org/volunteer or ask this assistant to send you the volunteer form.
Volunteers must be at least 16 years old.

**Are donations tax-deductible?**
Yes. As a registered non-profit, Amal Foundation issues official receipts that are
tax-deductible where local law allows.

**Can my company partner with you?**
Yes. We work with companies on corporate giving, sponsorship of scholarship cohorts, and
employee volunteering days. Email partnerships@amalfoundation.org to discuss.
